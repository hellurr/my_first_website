Organization Story

Our story began in 2018 when our founder traveled to Indonesia and came across tens and hundreds of stray animals, dogs and cats roaming around neighborhoods, streets, and beaches looking hungry, matted, dirty and hopeless. Being by herself, she knew she couldn't manage to do a lot for them except to feed as much of them as she would come across with wet pet food from the local stores that she stayed next to. Our founder took her experiences back to the U.S. with her and founded the Twin Cities Animal Rescue.
