Adoption Process

Bringing home a new family member is a big and important commitment, so you should be prepared to pour all your love and care into the tiny being you bring home.

But before that, first determine if your lifestyle from now to the next 10-15 years will be suitable to welcome a permanent furry resident.

Then, come and meet your purr-fect fur-iend that you are interested in and see if you are a compatible match! After that, submit an application and be ready for a home-visit from Twin Cities Animal Rescue worker to approve this move-in.

Once approved, congratulations, you are now one family member bigger in your household!

Foster Program

Fostering is another great way to contribute to the betterment of a shelter animal's life, as well as shouldering the stress that shelters carry due to the overcapacity of more animals than space.

To sign up as one of our foster parent members, apply and we'll send out a Twin Cities Animal Rescue worker to visit your home to ensure that you meet the criteria.

Once approved, come and meet a fur-iend that will be the best fit for you to take care of in our stead.

Volunteer Responsibilities

  • Socialize with our animals by walking a furry fur-iend of your choice, grooming them, and playing with them.
  • Feeding them at appropriate times of the day according to the animal's preferences, taking them out for potty breaks, etc.
  • Ensure we have tools and resources ready at hand, clean towels, bowls, gathering more supplies.
  • If applicable, needing to transport an animal with your own mode of transportation.
  • Go to rescue events as our representative, promote our rescue, raise funds, etc.
